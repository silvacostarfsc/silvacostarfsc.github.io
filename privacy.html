Privacy Policy

Pote a Pote · last updated October 30, 2026 · Versão em português

This policy explains how the Pote a Pote app ("the app"), developed by Raimundo Felipe da Silva Costa, handles player information.

Data stored by the app

Game progress (current level, records and preferences) is saved only on your device. The developer has no servers and does not receive this data.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob (videos, ads between levels and a banner). To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • the device advertising ID;
  • device and system information (model, language, version);
  • IP address and approximate location;
  • interactions with ads.

Google handles this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in your Android settings. Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are asked for consent before personalized ads.

Google Play Games

The game may use Google Play Games for achievements and leaderboards. Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Google receives your gamer name, unlocked achievements and scores, which may be shown to other players on leaderboards. Google handles this data under its own policy, and you can manage or delete your gamer profile in the Google Play Games app.

Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. The developer has no access to payment data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

You can delete all game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings. For questions or requests about your data, write to raimundofelipy@hotmail.com.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always published at this address.
